POWER CONTINUES. LIFE GOES ON.

Less Worry When the Grid Goes Down.

Standard grid-connected solar systems do not automatically provide power during a blackout. But when a system is specifically designed with compatible battery storage and backup capability, selected essential loads can continue operating.

💡
Lights Can Stay On

Keep selected essential spaces illuminated.

📶
Stay Connected

Support selected internet and communication equipment.

💻
Work Can Continue

Keep selected work devices operating during interruptions.

🏪
Business Can Continue

Backup selected critical business loads where appropriately designed.

👨‍👩‍👧
Family Life Continues

Maintain selected comfort, communication and entertainment loads.

🛡️
Greater Peace of Mind

Plan backup priorities before an outage happens.

Backup capability depends on system design, inverter configuration, battery capacity, load requirements and installation conditions.
